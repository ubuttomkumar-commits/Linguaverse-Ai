QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Good to know before you begin.

Is LINGUAVERSE AI suitable for beginners?

It is designed to support beginners with structured learning and clear explanations, while also offering pathways for more experienced learners.

Can I learn with Bangla explanations?

The product is designed with Bangla-speaking English learners as its initial audience. Actual language options depend on the features enabled in the live application.

Does it assess pronunciation?

Pronunciation feedback should only be provided when the connected speech technology supports reliable acoustic assessment. Speech transcription alone is not proof of pronunciation quality.

Can I delete my voice recordings?

Recording retention and deletion controls must follow the live product's published privacy policy. Review that policy before uploading personal audio.

Is the platform free?

The proposed model includes a free tier and optional premium features. Availability, prices, and usage limits must be confirmed in the live product.
